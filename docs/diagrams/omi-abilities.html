What Omi can do, and what it cannot do yet

A quick check of Omi against the big personal agents: what each one can do, where Omi is behind, where it is ahead, and what I would build first.

Quick check, 20 November 2026. From Omi's code and public pages. The other apps were not tested by hand, and what they offer in India can differ.

Omi already has the internet

Omi can search the web today. It is switched on in your local app. I ran Omi's own search on "GATE 2027 application form opening date" and it came back with the answer: the form opened on 2 September 2026 and closed on 12 October with the late fee.

So a wrong answer to a question like that is not a missing internet. It is one of these, and I have not tested which:

  • It did not search. The model decides on its own whether to search, and it can answer from memory by mistake.
  • It searched but read too little. Each search gives 4 extracts of about 600 letters. A date table gets cut in the middle. In my test, one result was cut right where the table started.
  • It cannot open the page. When the extract is not enough, Omi has no way to read the full page.
  • The question needs today's date. "When is the form open" has the answer "it already closed", which needs the model to compare dates.

Read it this way: the big four are ahead on almost every row about the outside world and about doing things. Omi is ahead on two rows only, and they are the two no one else has: reading a chat between two people, and finding you people.

What I would build, in this order

Not everything in the list. Only what a friend who knows you is expected to do.

  1. 1

    Make looked-up answers right

    Longer extracts, and let Omi open the best page when the extract is not enough. Also make sure it searches when the question is about dates, forms, prices or news. This is the GATE kind of question.

    smallweb_search tool
  2. 2

    Read a link you paste

    People paste links all the time. Today Omi sees only the address. The same page reader from step 1 does this.

    smallnew tool
  3. 3

    Remind me at a time I ask

    "Remind me at 6 to call mom." Every big agent has it and it is the most basic thing a personal agent does. Needs quiet hours first.

    mediumhub: remind-at-time
  4. 4

    See a photo or screenshot

    A screenshot of a chat, a form, a bill. Needs a reply model that can see, so it depends on the model choice.

    mediumneeds a check
  5. 5

    Voice, then links for bookings

    Both are already planned. They stay behind the four above.

    largehub: voice, bookings

What I would not build

Leave to the big apps

  • Long research reports.
  • Making pictures, slides and documents.
  • An agent that clicks through sites, logs in or pays. Our own research says use links the person taps.
  • Mail and calendar connections, for now. High trust cost, and Google and OpenAI give it free.

Where Omi should win

  • Knowing the person: memory with dates and people, shown openly.
  • Talking like a friend in Hinglish.
  • Reading a chat with someone and saying what is going on.
  • Finding people to talk to. Nobody else does this.

The point of the first list is not to beat ChatGPT. It is to stop Omi from failing at small things a friend with a phone could answer, so people do not leave for those.

How to keep this list honest

A table of features goes stale and does not tell us if Omi is right. A list of real questions does.

  1. 1

    Write 30 to 40 real questions, grouped by ability

    "When is the GATE form open?", "what is the score?", "what does this link say?", "remind me at 6", "best cafe near Indiranagar". Made-up asks, never from anyone's chat.

  2. 2

    Run them through the real reply path

    Same code a real message goes through, on a test user. Keep what happened: did it search, what it read, what it said.

  3. 3

    Mark each one and say why

    Right, wrong, or cannot do. For a wrong one, the reason: did not search, read too little, or no such ability. That gives a number per ability and tells us what to build.

  4. 4

    Add a question every time a real chat fails

    You hit one, it goes in the list. The list grows from real use, and we run it again after each change.
