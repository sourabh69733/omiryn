About

Friendship starts with being understood.

Omiryn is an AI you talk to. It learns your vibe, like what makes you laugh, what you believe, and what differences you can accept, then introduces you to people you'd get along with. Friends first.

What Omiryn is building

Omiryn builds a private understanding of you through conversation, then suggests a few friend introductions, each with a clear reason. The product is early, and feedback from real users shapes the roadmap.

What makes it different

  • Conversation before matching, so matches come from who you are, not a form.
  • Matched on what you can accept, not just what you have in common.
  • Human-readable safety, privacy, and consent expectations from the start.

Trust principles

Omiryn should tell users what AI is doing, ask before importing extra context, keep learned signals reviewable, and make support or deletion requests easy to start.
