AI helps Omiryn understand patterns. It does not replace your judgment.

Last updated July 29, 2026. This page explains where AI is used and what users should expect from it.

Where AI is used

Omiryn may use AI to reply in chat, summarize conversations, extract learned signals, organize memories, adapt tone, and support future compatibility suggestions.

What AI may get wrong

AI outputs can be incomplete, outdated, or inaccurate. Learned signals are inferred from context and should be reviewed before they are treated as important.

User control

  • You can choose whether to add memories or WhatsApp exports.
  • You can remove saved memories from your account where the feature is available.
  • You can contact us to request correction or deletion of personal data.

Human responsibility

Omiryn does not guarantee matches, relationships, chemistry, safety, or another person’s behavior. Users should make their own decisions and use care before sharing sensitive information or meeting anyone.

AI providers

Omiryn may use third-party AI providers to operate chat and learning features. We aim to send only what is needed for the requested feature and avoid intentionally storing raw private content in app activity logs.
