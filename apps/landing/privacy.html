Privacy Policy

Your data should make the product better without making you feel exposed.

Last updated July 29, 2026. This policy explains what Omiryn collects, why it is used, and how users can ask for correction, export, or deletion.

Information we collect

We may collect account email, profile basics, photos you upload, conversations with Omiryn, feedback messages, saved memories, WhatsApp exports you choose to import, public-site events, in-app activity events, invite requests, contact-form messages, and technical logs needed to run the service.

How we use it

  • To create and improve your compatibility profile.
  • To provide conversations, matching, onboarding, and support.
  • To understand which public pages, app pages, and actions are working.
  • To respond to feedback, invite requests, privacy questions, and safety reports.

AI and memories

Omiryn may use AI to reply in chat, summarize chats, extract learned signals, adapt tone, and support compatibility suggestions. Imported memories and WhatsApp text are used only when you choose to provide them.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not import WhatsApp unless you upload it.
  • We do not message people from imported chats.
  • We do not intentionally store full chat message text, memories, or WhatsApp content inside app activity event logs.
  • We do not want passwords, government IDs, financial data, intimate content, or content you lack permission to share.

Your choices

You can remove saved memories in the app and request correction, export, or deletion help through Contact. Some operational records may be retained where needed for security, abuse prevention, legal compliance, or service integrity.

Activity events and vendors

Public pages may record page views, CTA clicks, and form submissions with a browser session ID. The signed-in app may record limited activity events such as page views, chat opened, profile saved, memory imported, feedback submitted, invite requested, and client error codes. Hosting, database, auth, email, analytics, and AI vendors may process data to operate, secure, debug, and improve Omiryn.
