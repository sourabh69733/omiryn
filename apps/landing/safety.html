Safety Center

Clear rules for a respectful community.

Last updated July 29, 2026. Omiryn is for adults (18+) looking for new friends. Safety is product design, consent, privacy, and clear escalation paths.

User expectations

  • Users must be 18 or older.
  • No harassment, threats, hate, sexual pressure, impersonation, scams, or non-consensual sharing.
  • Respect boundaries immediately when someone says no, slows down, or stops responding.

Introductions and consent

Omiryn is designed around mutual approval before deeper connection. Users should not be pressured to move off-platform, share contact details, or meet in person before they are comfortable.

Imported context

Only import WhatsApp exports, memories, or notes that you have the right to share. Do not upload private third-party secrets, intimate content without consent, or information that could put someone at risk.

Report a concern

Send safety concerns through Contact. Include screenshots, conversation context, and the account or invite path involved. Urgent threats or immediate danger should be reported to local emergency services first.

AI limitations

AI can help find patterns and suggest compatibility, but it can be wrong. Users should make their own decisions, verify important claims, and avoid sharing sensitive personal information too early.
