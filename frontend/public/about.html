About

Dating should begin with being understood.

Omiryn is a conversation-first matchmaking product. Instead of asking people to perform through photos and bios, it learns values, tone, lifestyle, and intent through natural conversation.

What Omiryn is building

Omiryn helps users build a private compatibility profile through guided conversation, then uses that understanding to recommend fewer, higher-signal introductions. The product is early, and feedback from real users shapes the roadmap.

What makes it different

  • Conversation before matching, so preferences are based on lived context rather than shallow filters.
  • Small-batch introductions instead of endless swiping.
  • Human-readable safety, privacy, and consent expectations from the start.
