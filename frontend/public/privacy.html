Privacy Policy

Your data should make the product better without making you feel exposed.

Last updated July 16, 2026. This policy explains the practical privacy commitments for Omiryn’s early product.

Information we collect

We may collect account details, profile basics, conversation messages with Omiryn, uploaded context you choose to provide, product feedback, public-site analytics events, and contact details submitted through forms.

How we use it

  • To create and improve your compatibility profile.
  • To provide conversations, matching, onboarding, and support.
  • To understand which public pages and calls to action are working.
  • To respond to feedback, invite requests, and safety reports.

What we do not want

Do not submit passwords, financial information, government IDs, private third-party secrets, or content you do not have permission to share. Dating products are personal; only share what you are comfortable using for matching and support.

Your choices

You can request support, corrections, or deletion help through Contact. Some operational records may be retained where needed for security, abuse prevention, legal compliance, or service integrity.

Analytics

Public pages record page views, section views, CTA clicks, and form submissions with a browser session ID. This helps identify what visitors see before they join, without requiring a third-party tracker.
