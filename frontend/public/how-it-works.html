How it works

Three conversations. Better introductions.

Omiryn replaces swipe-first matching with a guided conversation that learns what matters, creates a private compatibility profile, and suggests people when there is stronger signal.

1

Talk naturally

Start with Omiryn like you would with a thoughtful friend. The product learns values, humor, rhythm, lifestyle, and relationship intent from the conversation.

2

Review what it learns

Your profile is private and editable. You can correct facts, give feedback, and shape what Omiryn should use for matching.

3

Meet fewer, better people

Instead of endless profiles, Omiryn aims for a small set of high-signal introductions where both people have reason to say yes.

What users see

Users see onboarding, chat, profile learning, match previews, feedback controls, and clear privacy, safety, and AI disclosure pages before they commit more personal context.

Why this matters

Dating apps often optimize for fast judgment. Omiryn is designed for slower signal: how someone thinks, communicates, handles values, and describes the life they want.

Control stays visible

Memories are user-provided, learned signals are reviewable, and contact paths are available for feedback, privacy questions, safety concerns, or deletion requests.
